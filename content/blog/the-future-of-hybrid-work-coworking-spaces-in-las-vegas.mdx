## Remote Work Doesn’t Mean WFH

Sharing a workspace is not just putting your desk next to another person. It’s about creating a healthy dynamic with other remote employees, freelancers and consultants in a space, making a comfortable, professional and productive atmosphere for everyone. Coworking is the balanced mix between autonomy and collaboration. Unlike a common office, coworking venues allow those who join them to manage their own time, priorities and pace. The main idea is to work in a stimulating space designed to not make you feel the constraints of a traditional workplace, or the boredom of a home office.

### Hybrid Working in Coworking Spaces

The limits that represent working from home, such as isolation, the blur in the lines between professional and personal life and a lack of social interactions, have inspired the creation of another format: [hybrid work.](https://www.esade.edu/beyond/en/what-is-the-hybrid-work-model-and-what-does-it-entail/#:~:text=What%20is%20hybrid%20work?,hours%20they%20will%20work%20remotely.) The balance of being present in a third space and having some days for working in your own office at home, gives employees the opportunity to taste the best of both worlds. Keeping the flexible hour schedule and the effects of socializing with other colleagues, represents a benefit for many professionals. Business leaders who want to implement hybrid working into their company model, see these spaces as a tool that helps them achieve all these benefits. They turn it into a natural extension of the office, where everyone can focus on sharing their ideas, have their meetings, commuting and boosting their productivity.

## What Coworking Spaces Offer To Remote Workers

### No more loneliness

Coworking places provide a good solution to the solitude that WFH can bring into a worker’s life, and still manages to respect the boundaries of your autonomy. Walking into a community of professionals that may share interests and experiences with you, can have an incredibly positive impact on your motivation and discipline to work.

### The environment improves your productivity

Being in a healthy, good environment can make a real difference in your productivity. Being stimulated with the company of other professionals, a comfortable office space and the lack of temptations that come with working at home, enhances your focus.

### Professional autonomy is easier to reach

Coworking spaces allow you to have a bit of external framework that you may miss at home, so staying on track with your daily tasks and goals is easier. When you work in a dedicated space, you have the opportunity to curate a routine without falling into the rigidity of a traditional office.  You set your own hours and activities, creating a structure that adapts to your daily life. Gaining autonomy while not missing out on the benefits of an office environment is a win-win.

### Choose when and where to work

One of, if not the most attractive advantage of coworking is the freedom to enter them at any hour of the day. It doesn’t matter if you’re an early bird, if you woke up late and had to update your work in the afternoon or if you prefer the night time.  If you have an emergency or a personal appointment to attend, you won’t necessarily miss out on your work, unlike the strict hour schedule of a normal office. You can manage your time according to your needs and daily pace, without worrying about your access to your preferred coworking space in Las Vegas.

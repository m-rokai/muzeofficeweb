## **Coworking Spaces = Employee Well-Being** 

Whether for a brainstorming session in a meeting room or a team-building retreat in a comfortable, fully-equipped space, the current trend in coworking spaces is undoubtedly focused on improving the well-being of remote workers. The managers of these spaces are increasingly seeking to create pleasant and functional environments, similar to those once found only in Silicon Valley. Among the most notable trends, we can mention:

-   Gradual disappearance of individual offices in favor of open-place spaces of different sizes, where teams can work together.
-   Elimination of fixed workstations assigned to each individual; the idea is to promote flexibility and allow each person to choose their workspace according to their preferences. Instead, lockers or storage cabinets with secure access are provided.
-   Growing presence of cafeterias and dining rooms to encourage interaction among colleagues.
-   Increasing number of relaxation areas (sofas, lounge areas, etc.), and more elegant and ergonomic furniture. The goal is to create a welcoming and comfortable environment.

At Muze Office, our coworking spaces are open for additional activities that promote employee well-being, such as yoga classes, informal gatherings, and training sessions.

## **A Work Solution With A Promising Future**

Here are some of the factors that have contributed to growing popularity of coworking spaces in recent years:

### **Financial Savings**

Entrepreneurs save a significant amount of money by not having to rent or purchase office space for their business. By choosing coworking, users benefit from flexible options and can rent space according to their needs. For businesses that don’t want to invest immediately to solve their space problems, coworking also represents a cost saving.

### **Talent Concentration**

While entrepreneurs and freelancers working in coworking spaces are focused on their own projects, these spaces naturally foster conversations and exchanges of ideas. This creates a diverse and enriching environment, where people with different backgrounds complement each other, share experiences, and support one another.

### **Time Savings**

Coworking spaces, usually located in strategic areas, allow employees to reduce their commute time. This contributes to their well-being and reduces stress.

### **Breaking Isolation**

When starting a project, it’s common for startup founders to work alone from home. This can lead to isolation in the long term, and coworking offers the opportunity to stay connected with other professionals.

The SEMA Show is the largest automotive aftermarket trade show in the world. Every November, over 160,000 manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and media professionals descend on the Las Vegas Convention Center for a week of product launches, buyer meetings, and deal-making that defines the industry's coming year.

The show floor is for demos and handshakes. The real work — supplier negotiations, contract reviews, distributor calls, and follow-up orders — happens off the floor. And the typical options during SEMA week are bad: hotel lobbies packed with attendees, business centers with outdated equipment, and convention-center "work lounges" where you can't hear your own phone ring.

Muze Office is 15 minutes south of the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Real desks, private meeting rooms, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why SEMA exhibitors and vendors need off-site workspace

SEMA is an order-writing show. The conversations that start at the booth get closed in a conference room. Specifically:

- **Supplier negotiations** require private space with a door that closes — not a booth where the competitor across the aisle can hear your volume pricing
- **Contract and order reviews** need a quiet desk, a printer, and enough time to read the fine print without someone interrupting to scan your badge
- **Distributor and rep calls** happen between floor shifts — your hotel room has bad acoustics and your booth has 80 dB of background noise
- **Team daily standups** for the exhibiting crew need a space where 4-6 people can debrief the day's leads without shouting

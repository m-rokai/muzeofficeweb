## What Are Virtual Mailboxes?

A virtual mailbox provides a physical business address where you can receive mail, which is scanned and transformed into secure digital files, making it available for you online. Businesses commonly use virtual mailboxes to manage new correspondence, but so do travelers, expats, and anyone who looks for a secondary place to receive their mail. 

When your mail arrives, the mail center workers will sort, scan, and upload it to a safe cloud space. After this, you will be allowed to manage your email from any device, no matter the time.

## Virtual Mailboxes vs. PO Box

Here’s a breakdown of the key differences between a [P.O. Box](https://mail.quadient.com/en/learn/po-boxes) and a virtual mailbox to help you decide which one suits your needs best.

### Physical Location vs. Digital Access

A Post Office Box, is a physical, lockable mailbox located at a post office. You’ll have to get to the post office to collect all you receive, which can be difficult if there’s traffic or if you don’t live close to the location. 

On the other hand, a virtual mailbox can give you digital access to your mail. This is a perfect setup for remote workers and businesses with multiple locations, since it offers the flexibility to administrate your mail without having to visit a physical office.

### Service Flexibility and More Features

While a PO Box is direct, limiting any services beyond basic mail collection, a virtual mailbox gives you a variety of additional features for streamlining mail management. With a virtual mailbox, you have the choice to open and scan your mail, and forward it to another address, shredded, or archived.

### Real Street Address Privilege

A PO Box offers you a box number and the post office’s address, which may not work for businesses that need a real street address. Usually, companies look for a registered street address for specific licenses, client trust, and official documentation.

A virtual mailbox can provide you with a real street address, and you can use it as your business or personal mailing address. This feature is especially good for businesses that want a trustworthy appearance, as it can establish credibility with clients and collaborators.

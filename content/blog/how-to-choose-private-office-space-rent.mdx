## **Renting a Private Office in a Coworking Space?**

[First featured in San Francisco in 2005](https://www.coworkingresources.org/blog/history-of-coworking), coworking remains the perfect solution for freelancers looking to develop their business outside the traditional corporate environment. They can enjoy a modern and creative workspace, be part of a community, and access multiple services previously out of reach of large companies.  Additionally, coworking centers offer a variety of office types, including private office spaces for those who need to conduct business with the utmost confidentiality.   We can talk about two great advantages when renting an office in a coworking space:

### Lower Cost

The first advantage of a private office is its lower cost, as this type of space can be rented for a specific period, depending on your needs. And while you might think that, in contrast to hot desks, renting a private office in a coworking space comes at a considerable cost, don’t forget that the rent includes reception services, internet access, and many other services you don't have to think about (charges, taxes, electricity, air conditioning, heating, cleaning, maintenance, etc.).

### Flexibility

The second advantage of renting a private office space is flexibility. In contrast to a standard lease, the space is rented on a monthly basis, and the contracts offered are more flexible. This is actually a true opportunity for workers who are looking to take advantage of a convenient environment to expand their business. Besides, by choosing to rent a private office in a coworking space, you also benefit from access to many common areas (reception, meeting rooms, relaxation area, etc.), allowing you to mingle with other client companies.

## **3 Things to Consider when Choosing a Private Office Space for Rent in Las Vegas**

There are several coworking spaces in Las Vegas, and it’s not always a simple decision to make. To find the ideal private office for your business, you can use the following criteria as a guide:

### 1\. Location Comes First

This is the most important factor. While some clients prefer to work in a coworking space located in the city center, others would require a quiet place where the public transport won’t interfere with their focus. Others will prefer [a location close to the airport](/blog/meeting-space-near-las-vegas-airport). This will depend on the client’s objective.

### 2\. What Services Are Offered

Not all coworking spaces offer the same services. Some offer well-being services, or sports classes, while others may offer training, coffee areas, [networking events](/las-vegas-event-space), and others. All these are factors to consider when you’re looking for the best private office in a coworking space.

### 3\. Do the Math

Price should also influence your choice of private office. Not all coworking spaces rent their private offices at the same cost. You must shop around for prices and choose the best option for your budget.

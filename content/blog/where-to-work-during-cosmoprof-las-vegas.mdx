Cosmoprof North America is the leading B2B beauty trade show in the Americas, and for three days every July it turns Mandalay Bay into the sourcing and dealmaking hub of the global beauty industry. The hard part for most attendees isn't finding a booth to visit — it's finding a quiet, professional place to actually write the order afterward.

The 24th edition of [Cosmoprof North America Las Vegas](https://cosmoprofnorthamerica.com/las-vegas/) is scheduled for **July 13–15, 2027 at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center**. The show brings beauty brands, manufacturers, suppliers, and retail buyers together for three days of sourcing and meetings.

Muze Office sits about 10 minutes from Mandalay Bay at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200 — off the Strip, near the airport, with real desks, private offices, AV-equipped meeting rooms, fast WiFi, and free on-site parking. Here's how to use it during Cosmoprof week.

## Why do Cosmoprof attendees need off-site workspace?

Beauty is a margin-sensitive, relationship-driven business. The show floor is built for first impressions; the purchase order gets written in a room with a door. A few realities push attendees off-site during the show:

- **Buyer presentations need privacy.** A brand founder pitching Ulta, Sephora, or a salon chain shouldn't be spreading samples across a hotel bed. A conference room with a screen and a clean table sells the line far better.
- **Negotiations take focus.** Contract manufacturers working out formulation, packaging, and minimum-order terms need quiet, not 80 decibels of show-floor noise.
- **Buyers compare on real spec sheets.** Reviewing pricing, MOQs, and delivery timelines across competing vendors takes a desk and a printer — not a folding chair at a booth.
- **Marketing teams need reliable WiFi.** Crews running social coverage and influencer coordination during the show need a stable connection and a quiet corner to edit and post.

If you expect to compare products, review terms, or speak with partners during the show, schedule time for that work away from the booth. A desk or meeting room gives you a place to follow up while the conversations are fresh.

## Meeting rooms and conference rooms for buyer appointments

For scheduled conversations — line presentations, buyer reviews, supplier negotiations — book a room by the hour instead of trying to talk over the crowd. Rooms include AV and video conferencing, so a buyer or partner who couldn't make the trip can still join the line walkthrough live.

A practical Cosmoprof move: block a conference room for a two- or three-hour window each afternoon, then funnel every "let's talk pricing for real" booth conversation into back-to-back slots. You walk the floor in the morning and close in a real room by mid-afternoon.

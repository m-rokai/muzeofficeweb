MAGIC is one of the fashion industry's largest trade events, and it runs twice a year in Las Vegas — once in February and once in August. The next edition, MAGIC Las Vegas, takes place **August 10–12, 2026, at the Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC)**, with show floor hours of 9 AM–6 PM Monday and Tuesday and 9 AM–4 PM Wednesday. A February edition follows in early 2027. It is a trade-only event: only verified fashion-industry professionals are admitted, not the general public.

MAGIC does not run alone. It is co-located with **PROJECT** (contemporary menswear — sportswear, denim, streetwear, footwear, and accessories) and **SOURCING at MAGIC** (manufacturers, suppliers, and supply-chain services), so the LVCC halls hold the whole apparel pipeline at once: more than 2,500 brands from 45-plus countries, retail buyers from boutiques up to chains and department stores, designers, sales reps, sourcing managers, and press. Organizers report that a large majority of attendees are senior decision-makers — buyers, owners, presidents, VPs, and C-suite — and that confirmed retail buyers in recent editions have included names like Nordstrom, Macy's, Bloomingdale's, Buckle, Boot Barn, Zumiez, and Tillys.

That attendee profile is exactly why off-site workspace matters here. MAGIC is a high-transaction show: buyers write orders on the spot, brand reps negotiate distribution and exclusivity terms, and designers pitch to retail buyers they would never get a meeting with outside the show. Order math, margin conversations, and contract terms are not things you want to handle at the back corner of a booth surrounded by competing brands — or in a hotel room with no table and weak WiFi.

Muze Office sits at **6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200**, in the Enterprise/Paradise area just south of the airport — about 10 minutes from the LVCC. It is a real staffed building with private meeting rooms, day-pass desks, fast WiFi, phone booths, an on-site cafe, and free on-site parking. Month-to-month, no lease, no membership required — just book a day pass online.

## Why MAGIC, PROJECT, and SOURCING attendees need off-site workspace

Show week compresses months of relationship-building into three days. Different roles hit the same wall — there is nowhere private and quiet to actually do the work:

- **Buyers reviewing line sheets and writing orders** need a quiet table to compare pricing, assortments, and delivery windows across brands without a rep hovering — and without losing the thread on a loud, crowded floor.
- **Brand and sales reps running back-to-back appointments** need a professional room to close, not a hotel lobby or a noisy restaurant. A meeting room with a screen lets them walk a buyer through the full line and pull up the order system on the spot.
- **Designers and product teams pitching retail buyers** need a conference room with a display for lookbooks and sell sheets — far more credible than a fold-out table mid-aisle.
- **Sourcing and merchandising teams** working SOURCING at MAGIC often need to loop in factories and HQ across time zones; a private phone booth or huddle room is the difference between confirming a PO and waiting until they fly home.

There is also a tax-and-incorporation angle that comes up at MAGIC specifically. A lot of emerging brands use the show to formalize US distribution, and Nevada is an attractive base — no state income tax, no corporate income tax, and no franchise tax on most small businesses. If you are an out-of-state or international brand who wants a Las Vegas business address to anchor that, a [Las Vegas virtual office](/las-vegas-virtual-office) gives you a real commercial address and mail handling from $39/mo without renting a full office.

It comes down to whether you are working solo or hosting someone.

MAGIC is the fashion industry's largest trade show. Twice a year — February and August — over 60,000 buyers, brand reps, designers, and retail executives gather at the Las Vegas Convention Center to review collections, place orders, and negotiate terms for the coming season.

MAGIC is a high-transaction show. Buyers write orders on the spot. Brand reps negotiate exclusive distribution terms. Designers pitch to department store buyers they'll never get a meeting with outside the show. That kind of deal-making needs private space — not the back corner of a booth surrounded by competing brands.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, real desks, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why MAGIC attendees need off-site workspace

The fashion trade show business moves fast, and MAGIC week compresses months of relationship-building into a few days:

- **Buyers reviewing line sheets and writing orders** need a quiet table, a printer, and enough time to compare pricing without a brand rep hovering
- **Brand reps scheduling back-to-back buyer meetings** need a professional setting that isn't their hotel room or a noisy restaurant
- **Designers pitching to retail buyers** need a conference room with a display for lookbooks and sell sheets — not a fold-out table at the booth
- **Merchandising teams calling HQ** need a private phone booth to relay order details, discuss margins, and get approvals

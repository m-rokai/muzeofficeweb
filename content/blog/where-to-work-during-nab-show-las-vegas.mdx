The NAB Show (National Association of Broadcasters) is the premier event for media, entertainment, and broadcast technology. Every April, over 65,000 content creators, broadcast engineers, filmmakers, podcasters, and media executives converge on the Las Vegas Convention Center to see new production technology, attend sessions, and close deals.

NAB attendees have a specific workspace need that most conventions don't: they work with large files. Video editors, podcast producers, and live streamers need upload-capable WiFi, real desks with room for external drives and monitors, and quiet spaces for recorded interviews or voiceover work. The average hotel room and convention center work area can't deliver that.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200 — with fast dedicated WiFi, private phone booths, and meeting rooms equipped for video conferencing.

## Why NAB Show attendees need off-site workspace

Media professionals don't stop producing during a convention. Between sessions and floor visits:

- **Video editors** need a real desk with fast upload speeds to cut footage, assemble sizzle reels, or push dailies to the team back home
- **Podcast hosts** need a quiet room — ideally with a door — to record interviews with guests they met at the show
- **Media buyers and sellers** need private meeting rooms for programming negotiations and deal terms that can't happen at a crowded booth
- **Production teams** need a space for daily standups where they can review footage on a screen larger than a laptop

## What Muze Office offers during NAB week

## Getting to Muze Office from the LVCC

- **Drive:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W, Bermuda Rd exit. Free parking on arrival.

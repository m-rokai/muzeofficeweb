## Purpose of the Partnership:

The purpose of this partnership is to unlock new opportunities for our members by integrating the best of both worlds - Muze Office's innovative workspace solutions and Equinox's unparalleled fitness and wellness programs. We believe that this collaboration will enhance our members' work-life balance, promoting a healthier, more productive lifestyle.

### The Unlocked Series by Equinox:

Muze Office members will gain exclusive access to Equinox's "[Unlocked Series](https://s.tiled.co/1ZppVf5/corporate-partnerships)" as part of this partnership. This unique program offers members the opportunity to experience one-of-a-kind Equinox programming in iconic venues across the country. Moreover, members can now unlock access to a specially curated collection of Equinox+ classes and content through a digital hub inspired by the original event series. This hub is exclusively available for members and can be accessed anytime, anywhere on demand.

## Benefits of the Partnership:

This partnership brings a host of benefits to our members:

### 1\. Exclusive Access:

Members will have exclusive access to Equinox's unique fitness programs and content, providing a holistic approach to wellness that complements their professional life.

### 2\. Flexibility:

With the digital hub, members can access Equinox+ classes and content anytime, anywhere. This flexibility allows members to incorporate fitness into their schedule seamlessly.

### 3\. Enhanced Productivity:

By promoting a healthier lifestyle, this partnership aims to enhance productivity. A balanced work-life routine contributes to improved focus and efficiency.

### 4\. Community Building:

The partnership fosters a sense of community among members, with shared access to exclusive events and programs. At Muze Office, we are committed to providing our members with the best experiences, and our partnership with Equinox is a significant step in that direction. We are excited about the unique opportunities this collaboration presents and look forward to seeing our members thrive in their professional and personal lives.

## Enjoy Exclusive Discounts:

As a valued member of Muze Office, we are delighted to offer you an exclusive benefit as part of our partnership with Equinox. In addition to gaining access to the unique fitness and wellness resources, you are also entitled to a special discount on Equinox+ classes and programs. This discount is our way of saying thank you for being a part of the Muze Office community. We understand the importance of a balanced lifestyle, and through this discount, we aim to make it easier for you to incorporate fitness and wellness into your daily routine. We hope you take advantage of this exclusive offer and enjoy the wide range of classes and programs available on the Equinox+ platform. Here's to a healthier, more balanced lifestyle!

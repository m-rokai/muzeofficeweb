## First Things First: What Is a Virtual Office?

Although it may sound contradictory, a [virtual office](/locations/las-vegas) is a brick-and-mortar space. Why is it called “virtual”, then? Businesses, freelance workers, and SMEs on a hybrid or WFH model have access to:

-   Live answering services: Professional receptionists answer calls in your company name, screen calls, take messages, and forward them according to your instructions.
-   Mail forwarding & management: Receiving physical mail and packages, with options for forwarding to your location, scanning contents, or holding for pickup.
-   Dedicated phone numbers: Providing a professional business phone number with call forwarding and management features.
-   Fax receiving & forwarding: Handling incoming faxes and delivering them digitally.
-   Professional business address: Using a well-known commercial address in a desirable location to enhance your company's image and credibility.
-   Business license address: In some cases, the virtual office address can be used for business registration and licensing.
-   Directory listing: Inclusion in an on-site directory, further solidifying your physical presence.
-   Private office rental (Hourly, Daily, or Monthly): Offering more dedicated temporary office space for focused work or client meetings.
-   [Event space](/las-vegas-event-space) rental: Larger spaces for workshops, seminars, or company gatherings.
-   Breakout areas & lounges: Informal spaces for networking or taking a break.

In sum, a virtual office in Las Vegas offers a flexible and cost-effective way to have the basic infrastructure and support services that a traditional office brings, but without the overhead expenses of running it. Therefore, the “virtual” aspect applies to the manner in which the services are used—often remotely.

## The Ultimate Benefit for Local Businesses: SEO

_Say Annie specializes in video content creation for the (growing) healthcare industry in Vegas—she prepares shootings for medical institutions and private practices. Although word of mouth and social media are serving her well, she needs to increase her visibility and connect with new prospective clients. Here is where the virtual office comes into play._ 

A virtual office address in Las Vegas can allow businesses to create or optimize their Google Business Profile listing in the area. As a direct result of that, it helps them appear in local search results when prospective clients search for the services they are offering. Because the address is verified by Google, when clients search for a specific service or product in the area, the provider will pop up, driving traffic to their website or contact. 

Additionally, a virtual office address is to be used consistently across different online directories and platforms, which is a crucial factor for local SEO. 

### **Boosting SEO**

For some businesses, a GBP listing goes hand in hand with a website. What many may not know, is that said site can be optimized to appear on the top of the search results pages of Google. Making localized content will make the engine recognize that the business is a reputable source for what they are offering. However, if the address on the site does not match the one on Google’s listing, it will likely be deemed irrelevant and therefore not be shown to users.

Las Vegas, the dazzling oasis in the Nevada desert, offers a plethora of unique experiences for visitors. Whether you're in town for business or pleasure, here are five must-do activities that will make your trip truly unforgettable. ![Tourists strolling on a sunny day with iconic castle architecture in Las Vegas](/images/blog/tourists-strolling-on-a-sunny-day-with-iconic-cast-2024-07-11-20-24-31-utc-scaled.jpg)

## 1\. Experience the MSG Sphere

The [MSG Sphere is Las Vegas](https://www.thesphere.com/shows/the-sphere-experience)' newest and most groundbreaking entertainment venue. Standing at 366 feet tall and 516 feet wide, it's the largest spherical structure in the world.This $2.3 billion marvel features a 16K resolution wraparound LED screen inside, covering 15,000 square meters - the largest high-resolution screen in the world. The exterior boasts 54,000 square meters of programmable LEDs, creating a stunning visual spectacle visible across Las Vegas. Inside, the 18,600-seat auditorium offers immersive experiences with cutting-edge audio technology, including beamforming and wave field synthesis. Whether you're attending a concert, watching an immersive film, or simply marveling at the exterior displays, the MSG Sphere is a must-see attraction that epitomizes Las Vegas' commitment to pushing the boundaries of entertainment.

## 2\. Visit the Fountains of Bellagio

No trip to Las Vegas is complete without witnessing the mesmerizing [Fountains of Bellagio](https://bellagio.mgmresorts.com/en/entertainment/fountains-of-bellagio.html). This free attraction features a stunning water show choreographed to music, with fountains shooting up to 460 feet high. The spectacle takes place every 30 minutes in the afternoons and early evenings, and every 15 minutes from 8 PM to midnight. It's a perfect way to take a break from work and enjoy a moment of awe-inspiring beauty.

## 3\. Explore the Fremont Street Experience

For a taste of old Vegas with a modern twist, head to the [Fremont Street Experience](https://vegasexperience.com/). This vibrant pedestrian mall in downtown Las Vegas is covered by a massive LED canopy that displays dazzling light shows. Street performers, live music, and unique shops add to the electric atmosphere. It's an excellent spot for team building activities or unwinding after a productive day at Muze Office.

## 4\. Take a Ride on the High Roller

For a breathtaking view of the Las Vegas Strip, hop aboard the High Roller, the world's tallest observation wheel. Standing at 550 feet tall, this massive Ferris wheel offers a 30-minute ride in spacious, climate-controlled cabins. As you ascend, you'll be treated to panoramic views of the city's iconic skyline, the surrounding desert, and on clear days, even the distant mountains.

## 5\. Visit the Neon Museum

Step into Las Vegas's glittering past at the [Neon Museum](https://neonmuseum.org/your-visit/). This outdoor museum, also known as the "Neon Boneyard," houses a collection of iconic Las Vegas signs from the 1930s to the present day. Take a guided tour to learn about the city's rich history and the stories behind these luminous relics. It's a perfect activity for those who appreciate design and nostalgia. Las Vegas continues to evolve and surprise visitors with new attractions like the MSG Sphere, while maintaining its classic charm with longstanding favorites.

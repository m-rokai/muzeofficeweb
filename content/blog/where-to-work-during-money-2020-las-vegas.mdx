## When and where is Money 20/20 USA?

Money 20/20 USA is held annually in October at the Venetian Expo, connected to The Venetian Resort on the Las Vegas Strip. According to the [official Money 20/20 website](https://us.money2020.com/), the 2026 edition runs **October 18-21, 2026**.

It's a smaller show than mega-conventions like CES or SEMA, but the per-attendee deal value is significantly higher — this is where partnerships, integrations, and funding rounds get negotiated. The official site reports the gathering draws **11,000+ senior attendees and 3,400+ companies from 85+ countries**, with roughly one in three holding C-suite roles. That's a concentrated crowd of decision-makers and a packed four days of back-to-back meetings.

## Why Money 20/20 attendees need off-site workspace

Fintech meetings involve confidential financial terms, valuation discussions, and regulatory-sensitive product details. A hotel lobby or a hallway corner near the keynote stage isn't the right venue. The real business happens away from the booth:

- **VCs and investors** taking pitch meetings need a private conference room where deal terms and valuations aren't overheard by the next meeting in line.
- **Payments and infrastructure companies** negotiating partnership and integration terms need AV-equipped space for technical demos and contract walkthroughs.
- **Startup founders** running multiple meetings a day need a home base between sessions to prep decks, follow up on leads, and take investor calls in private.
- **Compliance and legal teams** reviewing filings and contract language need a quiet desk where sensitive documents stay out of public view.

A purpose-built workspace a short drive from the Strip solves all of it: a door you can close, bandwidth that holds up under load, and an on-site cafe with hot meals when you don't want to fight the conference concessions.

Coworking spaces have been on the rise in recent years, and they're only going to become more popular in the next few years. By 2023, it's estimated that there will be over 30,000 coworking spaces around the world. That's a lot of people working side-by-side in shared offices! So how will coworking change the way we work in the future? Here are a few predictions:

## 1\. We'll be more productive.

As more and more people coworkers, they'll find that they're more productive when surrounded by other people. The hustle and bustle of a busy office can help some people focus and get their work done. And with all of the new collaboration tools being developed, it'll be easier than ever to get work done together without even being in the same room.

## 2\. We'll be more connected.

With so many people working near each other, we'll inevitably start to form closer bonds with our coworkers. We'll share ideas and information more freely, and we'll be able to rely on each other for support when needed. These relationships will extend beyond just our immediate coworkers; we'll also start to build relationships with people who work in different industries but share our coworking space.

## 3\. We'll have more flexible schedules.

As coworking becomes more popular, businesses will realize that they don't need to keep their employees chained to their desks from 9 to 5. With advances in technology, there's no reason why work can't be done from anywhere at any time. This flexibility will allow us to design our schedules and work when we're most productive.

## 4\. We'll take better care of ourselves.

When you have a regular 9-to-5 job, finding time to take care of yourself can be tough. But when you have a flexible schedule and other healthy people surround you, it becomes much easier to make time for exercise, healthy eating, and self-care. In a coworking environment, there's often an emphasis on wellness, so you can expect to see more yoga classes, fitness programs, and healthy snacks available. These are just a few ways that coworking will change how we work in 2023. As more and more businesses adopt this model, we can expect even more changes in how we live and work. Are you ready for the future of coworking?

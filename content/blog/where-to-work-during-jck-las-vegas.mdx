JCK Las Vegas is the largest jewelry trade event in the Americas, and for one long weekend it turns the Venetian Expo into the center of the global gem and watch business. If you are buying inventory, presenting a new collection, or closing six-figure orders, the show floor is for handshakes, not for the quiet, document-heavy work that actually moves deals forward. That work needs a private room.

JCK 2026 runs Friday, May 29 through Monday, June 1, 2026, at the Venetian Expo on the Las Vegas Strip, with select areas opening Thursday, May 28. The show is open to the qualified jewelry trade only, and credentialing is required. According to the [JCK Show](https://lasvegas.jckonline.com/en-us/about.html), the 2025 edition welcomed more than 17,360 attendees and 1,800 exhibitors, with roughly a quarter traveling from more than 100 countries. That is a lot of buyers and sellers competing for the same quiet corners.

Muze Office sits about 15 minutes off-Strip at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200, near Harry Reid International Airport. Private meeting rooms, fast WiFi, free parking, and an on-site cafe give JCK professionals a real place to do business away from a crowded booth.

## Why JCK attendees need off-site workspace

Jewelry deals are unusually sensitive. You are discussing grading reports, certification details, wholesale pricing, and margins that set a retailer's profit for the entire holiday season. None of that belongs in a conversation a competitor at the next table can overhear.

Here is who tends to book space during JCK week:

- Retail jewelers placing seasonal orders who need a quiet room to compare vendor pricing, check markup math, and call a partner or accountant before committing to a large purchase order.
- Designers and brands presenting new collections to major accounts, who want a conference room with a real display screen rather than a folding table next to the next exhibitor.
- Gemstone and diamond dealers negotiating bulk pricing and grading terms, who need privacy for high-value conversations and a secure place to talk numbers.
- Trade editors, buyers' agents, and jewelry content creators covering the show, who need a desk, dependable WiFi, and enough quiet to actually file their work.

The Venetian's public lounges and the show floor fill up fast, and neither offers the privacy a real negotiation requires. A booked room solves that for the hour or two you need it.

## How do I get there from the Venetian Expo?

- Drive: about 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo via I-15 south to I-215 west, Bermuda Rd exit. Free parking when you arrive.
- Rideshare: roughly $15 to $25 from the Venetian area, depending on demand during show week.

Because JCK packs the Strip and the surrounding hotels, the off-Strip drive is usually faster and far less congested than fighting for a table inside a convention hotel. Front desk hours are Monday through Friday, 10am to 7pm, so plan room bookings around the show's Friday-to-Monday schedule and reserve early — meeting rooms go quickly during a major convention week.

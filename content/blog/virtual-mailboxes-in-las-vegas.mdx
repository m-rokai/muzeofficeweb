A virtual mailbox gives your business a place to receive mail without leasing a full-time office. At Muze Office, that means a commercial mailing address at 6860 Bermuda Rd in Las Vegas, with mail handled by staff. Depending on your plan, you can pick up eligible mail, have it forwarded, or request scanning. Scanning is available as an add-on or included with some plans; ask the team which option fits your needs.

## What Muze Office provides

The [Las Vegas virtual office plans](/las-vegas-virtual-office) start with Mail Holding at $39 per month. It includes a business address with a suite number and notification when USPS letter mail arrives, with pickup during staffed hours. Sandstone at $69 per month adds package receiving from UPS, FedEx, and Amazon. Opal at $149 per month adds mail forwarding and workspace hours; Diamond at $249 per month adds more workspace hours and a local phone line.

Mail scanning is available, but it is not a standard feature of every plan. Contact Muze Office to confirm whether it is included with the plan you want or can be added separately.

## How to get started

1. **Choose the mail services you need.** If you only need letter mail notification and pickup, start with Mail Holding. Choose a higher tier if you need packages, forwarding, or workspace hours.
2. **Complete the required mail authorization.** USPS [Form 1583](https://about.usps.com/forms/ps1583.pdf) authorizes a commercial mail receiving agency to receive mail for you. Muze Office can explain its setup process and identity-verification requirements.
3. **Confirm the details before using the address.** Ask about suite assignment, pickup hours, forwarding, scanning, and any requirements imposed by a bank, agency, or platform where you plan to use the address.

## Is a virtual mailbox right for you?

It can be useful if you work remotely, travel often, or want business mail kept separate from your home address. If you need to read mail without visiting the office, ask specifically about forwarding and scanning. Those services depend on the plan or add-on you choose.

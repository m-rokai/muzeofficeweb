## **First Things First: What are Virtual Mailboxes?**

A virtual mailbox is a digital service that assigns you an address where your mail information is arranged, scanned, uploaded and stored, instead of it being physically delivered to your door. It is a great way for remote workers to organize business correspondence from anywhere in the world and still [prioritize privacy and security.](https://launchworkplaces.com/blog/how-to-get-a-virtual-address/) 

## **Benefits of Digitally Storing Your Mail**

-   **The whole world becomes your office:** Accessing and managing your mail from any location eliminates the common limitations linked to having a physical mailbox, like having to constantly visit the P.O. box. 
-   **Simplifies mail management:** As a centralized dashboard for all your packages or deliveries, digital mailboxes allow remote workers to collaborate more easily and efficiently. 
-   **Enhanced confidentiality:** As opposed to physical mailboxes that can be intercepted and easily susceptible to theft, digital ones offer better-quality security methods to ensure your information and sensitive documents aren't stolen or leaked.

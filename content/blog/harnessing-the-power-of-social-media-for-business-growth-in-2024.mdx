## Pick platforms that match how you sell

You do not need to be everywhere. For most local Las Vegas businesses and solo founders, two or three channels done consistently beat six done sporadically. A simple way to choose:

-   **Instagram and TikTok** — best for visual and short-form video brands: food, fitness, beauty, events, real estate, anything you can show rather than explain.
-   **LinkedIn** — best for B2B services, consultants, agencies, and out-of-state founders who registered a Nevada LLC and want to look established.
-   **Facebook** — still the workhorse for community groups, local service businesses, and event promotion in the Vegas valley.
-   **YouTube** — best if you can commit to longer-form video; it compounds over time and doubles as a search engine.

If your business is location-dependent, claim and optimize your Google Business Profile first. It often drives more local calls than any social platform, and it pairs directly with a real street address — something a [Las Vegas virtual office](/las-vegas-virtual-office) gives you without a long lease.

## Content is a production problem, not just an idea problem

Most people stall on social media not because they lack ideas, but because producing consistently from a kitchen table or noisy café is logistically annoying. Three practical fixes:

1.  **Batch your content.** Block one half-day, shoot a month of posts, then schedule them. Batching is far easier in a quiet, controlled space than in stolen 20-minute windows.
2.  **Standardize your setup.** Consistent lighting, a clean background, and a stable internet connection for uploads matter more than expensive gear.
3.  **Repurpose ruthlessly.** One long video becomes several short clips, a carousel, and a written post. Create once, publish many times.

For recording calls, podcasts, or voiceovers, a [phone booth](/las-vegas-coworking) or private room beats an open café where background chatter ruins the audio. Muze Office has enclosed phone booths and bookable [private offices](/las-vegas-private-office) for exactly this.

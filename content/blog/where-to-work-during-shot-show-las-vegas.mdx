## When and where is SHOT Show 2027?

SHOT Show (the Shooting, Hunting and Outdoor Trade Show) is produced annually by the National Shooting Sports Foundation (NSSF). According to the [official SHOT Show website](https://shotshow.org/), the next edition runs **January 19-22, 2027** across the **Venetian Expo and Caesars Forum** in Las Vegas — not the Las Vegas Convention Center, which trips up first-timers planning their routes.

The scale is hard to overstate. [NSSF reported](https://www.nssf.org/articles/48th-shot-show-closes-with-big-crowds-big-deals-big-innovation/) that the 2026 SHOT Show drew more than 53,000 industry professionals from all 50 states and 126 countries, with 2,744 exhibitors across more than 834,500 net square feet. That's a trade-only crowd — every attendee is a working buyer, seller, or media professional, which is exactly why so much of the real deal-making moves off the floor and behind a closed door.

## Why SHOT Show exhibitors need off-site workspace

SHOT Show is one of the industry's primary order-writing events, and the conversations that matter are about distribution agreements, dealer pricing, product exclusivity, and inventory commitments. That level of deal-making needs private space, not a noisy aisle. Between floor shifts, attendees consistently need somewhere quiet to regroup:

- **Manufacturer reps and distributors** negotiate dealer pricing and exclusivity terms that require a closed door and a real conference table — out of earshot of competitors.
- **Sales teams** need a daily standup space to review leads, assign follow-ups, and coordinate the next day's booth schedule.
- **Media and content teams** need quiet desks and upload-capable WiFi for writing product reviews, editing video, and scheduling post-show coverage.
- **Compliance and legal staff** reviewing industry-specific regulatory paperwork need private, distraction-free workspace.

A purpose-built workspace 10-15 minutes away solves all of it: a door you can close, bandwidth that holds up under a city full of conventions, and an on-site cafe with hot meals when you don't want to fight the convention concessions.

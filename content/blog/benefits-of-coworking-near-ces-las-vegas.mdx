CES runs every January at the Las Vegas Convention Center, drawing over 130,000 attendees from the global technology industry. It's the single largest convention Las Vegas hosts each year, and it completely overwhelms the city's workspace infrastructure for a week. Every hotel lobby becomes a makeshift coworking floor. Every business center has a line. Every cafe within walking distance of the LVCC is packed by 9am.

If you're exhibiting, attending meetings, or just trying to stay productive between sessions, you need a real workspace — not a hotel couch with a dying laptop battery and WiFi shared with 50,000 other guests.

## Why CES attendees need off-site coworking

CES is a show where the real work happens between sessions:

### Technology infrastructure that actually works

Our coworking space runs on dedicated high-speed WiFi — not a hotel network throttled to a crawl by tens of thousands of concurrent users. When you need to demo a product over video call, push code, or join a Zoom meeting with your team back home, the connection holds.

### A collaborative environment away from the floor

The show floor is for demos and handshakes. The coworking space is for processing what you learned, following up on leads, prepping tomorrow's pitch, and having real conversations with the people you met. Being around other focused professionals — not tourists — changes the energy of your workday.

### Flexibility that matches convention schedules

CES runs Tuesday through Friday, but most exhibitors are in town Monday through Saturday for setup and teardown. Our coworking space is open Monday through Friday, 10am to 7pm, with day passes ($25) that require no membership and no commitment. Come the days you need, skip the days you don't.

### An inspiring work environment that isn't a hotel room

Hotel rooms are designed for sleeping, not for spending 8 hours at a desk that's also a vanity. Muze Office has real desks, ergonomic chairs, natural light, phone booths for private calls, and an on-site cafe for actual meals — not a minibar.

### Increased productivity through focus

The LVCC work lounges fill up fast and offer zero privacy. At Muze Office, you can take a call in a phone booth, book a [meeting room](/las-vegas-meeting-rooms) by the hour for a client conversation, or just sit at a quiet desk and process the day's follow-ups without someone scanning your badge every 30 seconds.

### Easy access from the convention center

Muze Office is at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200 — about 15 minutes from the LVCC via I-15 S. Free parking when you arrive. Lyft or Uber runs $15-25 from the convention center.

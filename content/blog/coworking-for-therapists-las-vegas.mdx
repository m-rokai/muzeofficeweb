Therapists have used Muze Office as a place to work instead of leasing a traditional office or working from a larger facility. A private office offers a dedicated setting for administrative work, online appointments, and, when the room suits the practice, in-person sessions. The right setup depends on how you work and what privacy your clients need.

## Choosing a workspace for your practice

An open coworking desk can be useful for scheduling, writing, and other work that does not involve private client conversations. For sessions, look at a [private office](/las-vegas-private-office) with a door that closes. Ask to see the room before committing so you can assess its layout, noise level, and suitability for your practice.

Muze Office is at [6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200](/locations/las-vegas) in Las Vegas, with free on-site parking. The workspace includes WiFi and shared amenities. Private-office terms and availability vary by room, so contact the team for current options.

## In-person and online sessions

If you see clients in person, consider how they will arrive, wait, and enter your office. If you work online, check the room's internet connection and acoustics with the devices you use. You are responsible for selecting the privacy practices, technology, and workspace setup appropriate for your clients and professional obligations.

Muze Office provides workspace; it does not manage appointments, patient check-ins, records, billing, or clinical services. If you need a specific room layout or feature, discuss it with the team before booking.

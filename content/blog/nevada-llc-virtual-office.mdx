## What a Virtual Office Is (and Isn't)

A **virtual office** is a commercial business address you can use for your company even though you don't physically work there full-time. The core idea is that you get a real, verifiable street address at a commercial building, your mail is received and handled on your behalf, and — depending on the plan — you have on-demand access to the workspace for meetings or occasional work days.

What a virtual office **is**:

- A real commercial street address with a suite number
- A place where mail is received, sorted, and either held for pickup or forwarded to you
- Optional add-ons like mail forwarding, package receiving, meeting room credits, and coworking access

What a virtual office **is not**:

- A registered agent service (these are separate legal services — if you want to designate Muze Office personnel as your Nevada LLC's registered agent, the answer is no; you'll need a dedicated registered agent provider)
- A mail box rental at a retail mail center (those typically show up as "PMB" addresses, which some banks, payment processors, and Google Business Profile reject)
- Legal advice on how to structure your LLC

Being clear on those distinctions matters because the failure modes for each are different. A PMB address might get your Google Business Profile suspended. Missing a registered-agent filing can cause your LLC to fall out of good standing.

## Why a Real Commercial Address Beats a PMB

On paper, a private mailbox at a retail mail center looks similar to a virtual office — you pay a fee, you get an address, your mail goes there. The practical differences show up in three places:

**1. Bank account applications.** Business banking applications regularly ask for the physical location of the business. Banks flag PMB addresses (especially those at well-known mailbox retailers) and frequently require additional verification — or reject the application outright — when they appear. A commercial address at a multi-tenant office building looks like what it is: a real place of business.

**2. Google Business Profile.** A virtual-office-only address is not enough. Google requires the business itself to operate at the location, receive customers during stated hours, staff it with its own team, and maintain permanent signage.

**3. Credibility with clients.** "Suite 200" at a commercial building reads differently than "#215" at a retail strip center. For B2B founders selling to enterprise or mid-market customers, the address on a contract or a website footer is part of how the company gets evaluated.

None of these are abstract concerns. They come up in the normal course of running an LLC.

## What You Should Talk to a Nevada Attorney About

There are parts of forming and running a Nevada LLC that a virtual office does not solve for, and that are worth a conversation with a licensed Nevada attorney or CPA:

- **Registered agent designation.** Nevada requires every LLC to have a registered agent with a physical Nevada address. A virtual office is a business mailing address, not a registered agent service. You'll need to designate a separate registered agent — there are many providers in Nevada that do this for a small annual fee.
- **Economic substance and tax residency.** Simply having a Nevada address does not, by itself, make your business "a Nevada business" for tax purposes. States where you actually conduct operations may still claim tax jurisdiction over the company. This is squarely in the domain of a CPA or tax attorney.
- **Foreign qualification.** If you operate primarily in another state, that state may require your Nevada LLC to register as a foreign entity there. This varies by state and business activity.
- **Permits and licensing.** Some business activities require local Nevada licensing (Clark County business license, industry-specific permits). These have their own addressing and operational requirements that may or may not be satisfied by a virtual office.

These aren't reasons to avoid a virtual office for your Nevada LLC — they're reasons to talk to a professional so the virtual office fits cleanly into the rest of your setup.

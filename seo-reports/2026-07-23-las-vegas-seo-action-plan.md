# Muze Office Las Vegas SEO action plan

Date: July 23, 2026
Inputs: Google Search Console, BrightLocal, repository audit, public entity/listing review, and three-agent consultant debate

## Executive conclusion

Muze Office does not have a technical-indexing emergency or a shortage of generic blog posts. The website is technically strong and Google Search Console shows substantially more search visibility than Ahrefs' free database reports. The binding constraints are:

1. fragmented business identity and stale third-party facts;
2. only 11 Google reviews and a weak citation footprint;
3. informational articles sometimes owning commercial queries;
4. incomplete source attribution between the website and Optix;
5. core workspace pages being one level too deep in the primary navigation.

Do not buy Ahrefs, Semrush, or another recurring keyword subscription this month. Do not publish another broad batch of generic coworking articles.

## 30-day action sequence

### 1. Approve the canonical business-data sheet

Owner must confirm:

- exact real-world business name as displayed on permanent signage;
- exact live GBP primary category;
- exact public hours versus 24/7 member-access terms;
- exact GBP map pin coordinates;
- current prices and booking links.

Then align the website schema, GBP, Optix, and existing listings.

### 2. Correct existing high-impact profiles for free

2. LinkedIn
3. Yelp
4. CoworkingCafe
5. LANS
6. Peerspace

10. the Clark County phone typo, if the issuing office accepts corrections

### 3. Run a compliant review workflow

Ask every genuine completed booking and new member for an honest Google review, using the same neutral request for everyone. Do not offer incentives and do not gate requests based on satisfaction.

Targets:

- 15–18 total reviews by day 30
- 20–25 by day 60
- 30+ by day 90

### 5. Measure on days 14 and 30

Use:

- Google Search Console for query/page ownership, clicks, impressions, CTR, and position;
- BrightLocal grids for the five tracked local intents;
- GBP Performance for calls, website clicks, directions, and discovery;
- Vercel conversion events plus Optix booking/member aggregates for outcomes;
- the optional self-reported source field to distinguish Google Search, Maps, AI assistants, directories, and referrals.

## Success thresholds

By day 30:

- every major owned/existing listing uses the approved identity;
- at least 15 genuine Google reviews;
- at least 15 accurate citations submitted or live;
- Day Pass begins receiving impressions for its intended commercial queries;
- meeting-room commercial-page ownership improves from the current weak baseline;
- every new website inquiry carries first-touch attribution.

Do not judge success from Ahrefs' keyword count alone.

ISC West is the largest physical security trade show in North America. Every March or April, over 30,000 security integrators, installers, end users, and manufacturers gather at the Venetian Expo in Las Vegas to see the latest in surveillance, access control, cybersecurity, fire and life safety, and connected-home technology.

Security industry professionals often work with sensitive information — client site plans, proprietary system architectures, bid proposals — that can't be reviewed on a show floor or in a hotel lobby. ISC West attendees need private workspace more than most.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why ISC West attendees need off-site workspace

- **Security integrators** reviewing project bids and client proposals need a private desk where sensitive information isn't visible to passersby
- **Manufacturers meeting dealer prospects** need a conference room for technical presentations and system demos on a large screen
- **Sales engineers** need quiet space for customer follow-up calls and proposal writing between floor visits
- **End users evaluating products** need a desk to compare spec sheets, review pricing, and consult with their internal teams before placing orders

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** Huddle Room ($25/hr, 2-4 people), Conference Room ($50/hr, 6-8 people), Boardroom ($75/hr, 10-14 people). All include AV, video conferencing, and whiteboards.

ASD Market Week is the most comprehensive retail trade show in the U.S. Twice a year — March and August — over 45,000 retail buyers, importers, distributors, and manufacturers converge on the Las Vegas Convention Center to source products across every consumer category: beauty, fashion accessories, home goods, electronics, toys, health and wellness, and general merchandise.

ASD is a sourcing show. Buyers are there to find new products, negotiate volume pricing, and write purchase orders. Vendors are there to close new accounts. That volume of transaction activity needs private meeting space — which the show floor and the Strip hotels can't provide at scale.

Muze Office is 15 minutes south at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, coworking desks, and free parking.

## Why ASD attendees need off-site workspace

ASD is high-velocity: buyers schedule 10-15 vendor meetings per day. Between those meetings:

- **Retail buyers** need a quiet desk to review samples, compare pricing sheets, and calculate margins before committing to purchase orders
- **Vendors and sales reps** need a conference room for follow-up presentations with buyers who want a deeper dive than the booth allows
- **Sourcing teams** need private space to call their warehouse, logistics, or finance team for approval on large orders
- **International exhibitors** need reliable WiFi for video calls back to their home office in a different time zone

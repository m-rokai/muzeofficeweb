CES brings over 130,000 attendees to the Las Vegas Convention Center every January. For a week, every Strip hotel lobby becomes an unofficial coworking space — packed with badge-wearing attendees taking calls on couches, fighting over outlets, and trying to hold Zoom calls over the lobby music. If you're exhibiting, closing deals, or just trying to stay on top of email between sessions, working from the convention floor or your hotel room gets old fast.

Muze Office sits at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200 — about 15 minutes from the LVCC via I-15 South. It's an actual workspace with real desks, fast WiFi, private phone booths, and meeting rooms you can book by the hour. Here's how to use it during CES week.

## Why CES attendees need off-site workspace

CES is a show where the deals happen between sessions, not on the show floor. Exhibitors need private space to:

- **Take investor or partner calls** without the PA system announcing the next keynote in the background
- **Prep demos and pitches** for the next day's meetings in a quiet room with a real desk
- **Host private meetings with buyers or press** in a conference room with AV equipment — not a hotel hallway alcove
- **Process follow-up emails and orders** after a day on the floor, when the hotel business center has a 20-person line

The LVCC itself has limited workspaces that get mobbed within minutes of opening. Your hotel room has bad WiFi, a tiny desk, and housekeeping knocking at noon. The Priority Pass lounge is standing room only during CES. You need an actual workspace.

## Getting to Muze Office from the LVCC

The Las Vegas Convention Center is at the north end of the Strip near the intersection of Paradise Rd and Convention Center Dr. Muze Office is about 15 minutes south via I-15 S to I-215 W, exit at Bermuda Rd.

- **Driving:** 15 minutes from the LVCC. Free parking when you arrive — no garage fees.
- **Rideshare:** Lyft or Uber from the LVCC typically runs $15-25 depending on CES surge pricing.
- **From your hotel:** If you're staying on the central Strip, it's 10-15 minutes via I-15 S or Las Vegas Blvd.

The drive is worth it. While Strip garages charge $35-60 during CES, Muze Office parking is free for all visitors.

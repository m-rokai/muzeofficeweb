CONEXPO-CON/AGG is the largest construction trade show in North America. Held every three years in March at the Las Vegas Convention Center (with the most recent editions drawing over 130,000 attendees), it brings together equipment manufacturers, contractors, aggregate producers, and construction technology companies for a week of demos, deal-making, and industry education.

The show sprawls across the entire LVCC campus including outdoor demo areas — which means long walks between meetings, extreme noise levels during live equipment demonstrations, and zero quiet workspace on the convention grounds. If you need to close a deal, review a quote, or take a call with your home office, you need to leave the venue.

Muze Office is 15 minutes south at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, quiet desks, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why CONEXPO attendees need off-site workspace

Construction industry deals at CONEXPO often involve six- and seven-figure equipment purchases. The paperwork and negotiations don't happen at the booth:

- **Equipment buyers** need private space to review quotes, compare financing terms, and call back to their operations team for approval
- **Manufacturers and dealers** need a conference room for distributor negotiations that can't happen within earshot of competitors
- **Project managers and estimators** need a quiet desk to run numbers and build proposals based on what they saw on the floor
- **Marketing and media teams** covering the show need upload-capable WiFi and editing desks for video content and social media

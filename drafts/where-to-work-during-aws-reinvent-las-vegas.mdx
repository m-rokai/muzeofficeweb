AWS re:Invent is Amazon Web Services' annual cloud computing conference. Every December, over 50,000 cloud engineers, DevOps leads, CTOs, solution architects, and AWS partners take over the Venetian Expo and multiple Strip venues for a week of sessions, certifications, partner meetings, and product announcements.

re:Invent attendees are tech professionals who need real infrastructure to work: fast upload/download speeds for pushing code and accessing cloud dashboards, reliable WiFi that doesn't throttle during peak hours, and quiet space for focused work. The Venetian's WiFi crumbles under 50,000 concurrent users, and hotel rooms are designed for sleeping, not for running `kubectl` commands.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Dedicated fast WiFi, real desks, phone booths, and meeting rooms.

## Why re:Invent attendees need off-site workspace

- **Engineers and architects** need reliable, fast WiFi to access AWS consoles, review infrastructure, and push code — not the throttled hotel network shared with 50,000 other guests
- **CTOs and VPs of engineering** taking partner and vendor meetings need a private conference room with video conferencing — not a hotel suite converted into a "meeting space"
- **Solutions architects** working on customer proof-of-concept demos need a quiet desk with a stable connection
- **DevRel and content teams** writing blog posts, editing session recordings, or live-streaming recaps need upload-capable bandwidth and a quiet environment

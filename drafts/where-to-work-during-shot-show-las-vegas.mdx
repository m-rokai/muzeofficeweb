The SHOT Show (Shooting, Hunting and Outdoor Trade Show) is the firearms and outdoor industry's largest trade event. Every January, over 60,000 manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and media professionals fill the Venetian Expo and Las Vegas Convention Center for product launches, dealer meetings, and order writing.

SHOT Show is a closed, trade-only event — every attendee is a working professional. The conversations are about distribution agreements, dealer pricing, product exclusivity, and inventory commitments. That level of deal-making needs private space, not a convention floor where competitors are one aisle over.

Muze Office is 15 minutes south at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms with AV, real desks, and free parking — away from the show floor and the Strip.

## Why SHOT Show exhibitors need off-site workspace

SHOT Show is the industry's primary order-writing event. Between floor shifts:

- **Manufacturer reps and distributors** negotiate dealer pricing and exclusivity terms that require a closed door and a real conference table
- **Sales teams** need a daily standup space to review leads, assign follow-ups, and coordinate the next day's booth schedule
- **Media and content teams** need quiet desks for writing product reviews, editing video content, and scheduling post-show publication
- **Compliance and legal teams** reviewing firearms-industry-specific regulatory paperwork need private, quiet workspace

## Getting there

- **From the Venetian Expo / LVCC:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W, Bermuda Rd exit.
- **Parking:** Free at Muze Office. Convention parking at the Venetian runs $20-40.

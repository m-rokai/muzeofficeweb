The Global Gaming Expo (G2E) is the gaming industry's premier trade event. Every October, over 25,000 casino operators, gaming technology companies, regulators, tribal gaming representatives, and industry suppliers gather at the Venetian Expo in Las Vegas. G2E is where new slot machines, table game technology, sports betting platforms, and iGaming solutions get demonstrated, evaluated, and contracted.

Gaming industry deals often involve regulatory review, licensing considerations, and significant capital commitments. The conversations that start on the G2E show floor — between a casino operator and a technology vendor, or between a sports betting platform and a content provider — need private space to develop.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200.

## Why G2E attendees need off-site workspace

- **Casino operators** evaluating gaming technology need a private conference room for detailed product demos and pricing negotiations
- **Gaming technology companies** meeting multiple casino operators per day need a professional meeting space outside their hotel room
- **Regulatory and compliance teams** reviewing licensing paperwork need a private, quiet desk
- **Sports betting and iGaming companies** negotiating content and data partnerships need AV-equipped meeting rooms for platform demos

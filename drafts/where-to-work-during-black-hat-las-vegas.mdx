Black Hat USA is the cybersecurity industry's most influential event. Every August, over 20,000 security researchers, CISOs, penetration testers, vendor sales engineers, and government security professionals gather at the Mandalay Bay Convention Center in Las Vegas for briefings, training sessions, and an expo hall filled with security vendors.

Black Hat attendees — more than almost any other convention audience — care about network security and privacy. Working from hotel WiFi or the convention center network is, for this crowd, actively uncomfortable. Cybersecurity pros need a network they can trust and a room where sensitive client work isn't visible to the person at the next table.

Muze Office is about 10 minutes from Mandalay Bay at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Dedicated WiFi, private phone booths, and meeting rooms.

## Why Black Hat attendees need off-site workspace

- **Security consultants and pentesters** working on client engagements between sessions need a secure, private desk — not a hotel lobby where a screen with client vulnerability data is visible to anyone walking by
- **CISOs and security directors** need private meeting rooms for vendor evaluations and internal strategy calls
- **Vendor sales teams** need a professional conference room for prospect demos that goes beyond a booth-level pitch
- **Researchers** preparing or rehearsing presentations need quiet space with reliable internet

## What Muze Office offers during Black Hat week

**Day Pass — $25.** Private desk, dedicated WiFi, coffee, free parking, phone booths.

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** Huddle Room ($25/hr) for vendor 1-on-1s. Conference Room ($50/hr) for team syncs and demos. Boardroom ($75/hr) for executive sessions.

## Getting there from Mandalay Bay

- **Drive:** About 10 minutes via Las Vegas Blvd south or I-15 S to I-215 W. Free parking.
- **Rideshare:** $10-20 from Mandalay Bay.

Black Hat runs every August, followed immediately by DEF CON. Reserve meeting rooms in advance during security week — the overlap makes Las Vegas particularly busy.

HLTH is one of the fastest-growing healthcare innovation events in the country. Every October, over 12,000 health-tech founders, hospital system executives, digital health investors, pharma representatives, and health-plan leaders gather at the Venetian Expo in Las Vegas to discuss the future of healthcare delivery, technology, and financing.

HLTH is a high-density networking event — attendees schedule 15-20 meetings in three days. The conversations span investment terms, pilot programs, integration partnerships, and regulatory strategy. That volume of high-stakes meetings demands professional meeting space, not a coffee bar in the Venetian lobby.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200.

## Why HLTH attendees need off-site workspace

- **Health-tech founders** pitching to investors and health systems need a private conference room with AV for product demos — not a 10-minute slot at a crowded booth
- **Digital health investors** conducting due-diligence meetings need private space for financial review and term-sheet discussions
- **Hospital system innovation teams** evaluating technology vendors need a quiet conference room for detailed product walkthroughs
- **Pharma and health-plan representatives** negotiating partnership terms need privacy for sensitive commercial conversations

## What Muze Office offers during HLTH

**Day Pass — $25.** Desk, fast WiFi, coffee, free parking, phone booths.

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** Huddle Room ($25/hr) for investor 1-on-1s. Conference Room ($50/hr) for product demos. Boardroom ($75/hr) for executive negotiations. AV and video conferencing included.

## Getting there from the Venetian Expo

- **Drive:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W, Bermuda Rd exit. Free parking.
- **Rideshare:** $15-25 from the Venetian.

HLTH runs every October, often adjacent to G2E and Money 20/20. Reserve meeting rooms early — October is one of the busiest convention months in Las Vegas.

JCK Las Vegas is the jewelry industry's flagship trade event in the Americas. Every June, over 20,000 retail jewelers, designers, gemstone dealers, watch brands, and suppliers gather at the Venetian Expo to source inventory, launch new collections, and negotiate terms for the holiday selling season.

Jewelry transactions involve high-value merchandise, specific grading and certification details, and pricing negotiations that can define a retailer's margins for the year. That kind of business requires a professional meeting environment, not a booth table surrounded by competing vendors.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why JCK attendees need off-site workspace

- **Retail jewelers** placing seasonal orders need a quiet space to review vendor pricing, compare markup margins, and call their accountant before committing to large purchase orders
- **Designers and brands** presenting new collections to major retailers need a conference room with a large display — not a fold-out table next to the next booth
- **Gemstone dealers** negotiating bulk pricing and grading terms need privacy for high-value conversations
- **Trade publication editors and jewelry influencers** writing show coverage need a desk with reliable WiFi and enough quiet to focus

## What Muze Office offers during JCK

**Day Pass — $25.** Desk, WiFi, coffee, free parking, phone booths.

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** Huddle Room ($25/hr, 2-4 people), Conference Room ($50/hr, 6-8 people), Boardroom ($75/hr, 10-14 people). AV and video conferencing included.

## Getting there from the Venetian Expo

- **Drive:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W, Bermuda Rd exit. Free parking.
- **Rideshare:** $15-25 from the Venetian area.

JCK Las Vegas runs every June. Reserve meeting rooms in advance during show week.

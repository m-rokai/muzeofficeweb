MJBizCon is the largest cannabis industry trade show in the world. Every November, over 35,000 cultivators, manufacturers, dispensary operators, investors, attorneys, and ancillary service providers fill the Las Vegas Convention Center. The cannabis industry moves fast, and MJBizCon is where deals, partnerships, and investments close in-person.

Cannabis business is complex — it spans regulated product, real estate, licensing, banking, and compliance. That means the meetings happening off the show floor often involve sensitive financial details, licensing terms, and legal review. A hotel lobby or a restaurant table isn't the right venue.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Private meeting rooms, quiet coworking, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why MJBizCon attendees need off-site workspace

- **Investors meeting founders** need a private conference room for pitch meetings and financial review — not a booth where adjacent exhibitors can overhear valuation discussions
- **Multi-state operators** need a quiet desk to coordinate with compliance teams across multiple jurisdictions during the show
- **Attorneys and consultants** reviewing licensing paperwork or deal terms need private space with a real table
- **Dispensary buyers** placing product orders need to call their purchasing team for approval without the show floor in the background

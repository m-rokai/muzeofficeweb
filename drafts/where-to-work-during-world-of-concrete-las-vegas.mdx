World of Concrete is the largest annual international event for the commercial concrete and masonry industries. Every January — often running the same week as or adjacent to CES — over 60,000 concrete contractors, equipment manufacturers, material suppliers, and construction professionals pack the Las Vegas Convention Center for demos, education sessions, and deal-making.

The show floor is massive (700,000+ sq ft of exhibits), loud, and built for product demos, not for paperwork. When you need to review a quote, take a private call with your office, or sit down with a supplier for a real contract conversation, the floor isn't the place.

Muze Office is 15 minutes south of the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200 — real desks, private meeting rooms, free parking, and none of the concrete dust.

## Why World of Concrete attendees need off-site workspace

Construction professionals tend to do business face-to-face, and the deals at WOC often involve significant purchase orders, multi-year equipment contracts, and supply agreements. You need private space for:

- **Reviewing bids and quotes** from vendors you met on the floor — not on a hotel bed with your laptop balanced on a pillow
- **Supplier negotiations** that require a conference table, not a cocktail bar
- **Calls back to the home office** to get approval on a large order — your foreman doesn't need to hear the show floor in the background
- **Printing and scanning contracts** that need real signatures before the show ends

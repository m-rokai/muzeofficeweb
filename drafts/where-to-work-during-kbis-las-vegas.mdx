The Kitchen & Bath Industry Show (KBIS) is the premier trade event for the kitchen and bath industry. Every February, over 30,000 designers, architects, builders, dealers, and manufacturers gather at the Las Vegas Convention Center to see new products, attend design presentations, and source materials for upcoming projects.

KBIS attendees are visual professionals — interior designers, kitchen planners, bathroom remodelers — who need workspace that accommodates design review, not just email. When you're comparing product finishes on a laptop screen between meetings, a hotel room desk doesn't cut it.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200. Real desks, conference rooms with large displays, fast WiFi, and free parking.

## Why KBIS attendees need off-site workspace

- **Designers and architects** reviewing product samples and specifications need a real desk with enough room to spread out catalogs, material samples, and a laptop simultaneously
- **Builders and dealers** writing orders and comparing vendor pricing need a quiet environment where they can run numbers without interruption
- **Manufacturer reps** scheduling back-to-back meetings with dealers need a conference room with a large display for product presentations
- **Design firm teams** attending KBIS together need a standup space to debrief the day's finds and coordinate next-day priorities

## What Muze Office offers during KBIS

**Day Pass — $25.** Desk, WiFi, coffee, parking, phone booths. No membership required.

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** Huddle Room ($25/hr), Conference Room ($50/hr) with large display for visual presentations, Boardroom ($75/hr). AV included.

## Getting there from the LVCC

- **Drive:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W, Bermuda Rd exit. Free parking.
- **Rideshare:** $15-25 from the LVCC.

KBIS often co-locates with IBS (International Builders' Show), making the combined event even larger. Reserve meeting rooms in advance during show week.

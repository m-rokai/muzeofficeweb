Cosmoprof North America is the leading B2B beauty trade show in the Americas, and for three days every July it turns Mandalay Bay into the sourcing and dealmaking hub of the global beauty industry. The hard part for most attendees isn't finding a booth to visit — it's finding a quiet, professional place to actually write the order afterward.

The 2025 edition drew roughly **26,000 attendee visits from 103 countries** and more than **1,068 exhibitors**, according to organizer Cosmoprof — a lot of cosmetics manufacturers, skincare and hair-care brands, retail buyers, and packaging suppliers packed into one hall, and a lot of conversations that need to move somewhere private to close.

## Why do Cosmoprof attendees need off-site workspace?

Beauty is a margin-sensitive, relationship-driven business. The show floor is built for first impressions; the purchase order gets written in a room with a door. A few realities push attendees off-site during the show:

- **Buyer presentations need privacy.** A brand founder pitching Ulta, Sephora, or a salon chain shouldn't be spreading samples across a hotel bed. A conference room with a screen and a clean table sells the line far better.
- **Negotiations take focus.** Contract manufacturers working out formulation, packaging, and minimum-order terms need quiet, not 80 decibels of show-floor noise.
- **Buyers compare on real spec sheets.** Reviewing pricing, MOQs, and delivery timelines across competing vendors takes a desk and a printer — not a folding chair at a booth.
- **Marketing teams need reliable WiFi.** Crews running social coverage and influencer coordination during the show need a stable connection and a quiet corner to edit and post.

The stakes are high because the category is enormous. McKinsey's [analysis of the global beauty industry](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/a-close-look-at-the-global-beauty-industry-in-2025) projects the core market — skin care, cosmetics, hair care, and fragrance — to reach roughly **$590 billion by 2030**, growing about 5 percent a year. During Cosmoprof week, a meaningful slice of that year's sourcing decisions get made in three days, and the brands that come prepared with their own workspace close more of them.

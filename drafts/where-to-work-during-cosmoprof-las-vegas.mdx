Cosmoprof North America is the leading B2B beauty trade event in the Americas. Every July, over 35,000 cosmetics manufacturers, skincare brands, hair care companies, beauty retail buyers, and packaging suppliers fill the Las Vegas Convention Center for sourcing, trend discovery, and deal-making.

Beauty is a margin-sensitive, relationship-driven industry. Buyers evaluate formulations, packaging quality, and pricing in person — and then the follow-up negotiations need private space. A booth on the show floor is great for first impressions. A conference room off the floor is where the purchase order gets written.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the LVCC at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200.

## Why Cosmoprof attendees need off-site workspace

- **Beauty brand founders** meeting retail buyers (Ulta, Sephora, salon chains) need a professional conference room for line presentations — not their hotel room with samples spread on the bed
- **Contract manufacturers** negotiating formulation and packaging terms need private, quiet meeting space
- **Retail buyers** comparing vendor pricing, minimum order quantities, and delivery timelines need a real desk to review spec sheets
- **Marketing teams** coordinating social media coverage and influencer partnerships during the show need reliable WiFi and a quiet environment

## What Muze Office offers during Cosmoprof

**Day Pass — $25.** Desk, WiFi, coffee, free parking, phone booths.

**Meeting Rooms — from $25/hr.** All rooms include AV and video conferencing. Huddle Room ($25/hr), Conference Room ($50/hr), Boardroom ($75/hr).

## Getting there

- **From the LVCC:** 15 minutes via I-15 S to I-215 W. Free parking.
- **Rideshare:** $15-25 from the convention center.

Cosmoprof North America runs every July. Reserve meeting rooms in advance during show week.

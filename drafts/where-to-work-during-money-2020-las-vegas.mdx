Money 20/20 is the world's largest fintech event. Every October, over 13,000 payments executives, banking innovators, venture capitalists, and startup founders gather at the Venetian Expo in Las Vegas. The attendance is smaller than mega-shows like CES or SEMA, but the per-attendee deal value is significantly higher — this is where payment rails, partnerships, and funding rounds get negotiated.

Fintech meetings involve confidential financial terms, valuation discussions, and regulatory-sensitive product details. A hotel lobby or a Venetian hallway corner isn't the right venue. Money 20/20 attendees need professional, private meeting space away from the show floor.

Muze Office is 15 minutes from the Venetian Expo at 6860 Bermuda Rd, Suite 200.

## Why Money 20/20 attendees need off-site workspace

- **VCs and investors** taking pitch meetings need a private conference room where deal terms and valuations aren't overheard by the next pitch in line
- **Payments companies** negotiating partnership and integration terms need AV-equipped meeting space for technical presentations
- **Startup founders** attending multiple meetings per day need a home base between sessions to prep decks, follow up on leads, and take calls
- **Compliance and legal teams** reviewing regulatory filings and contract terms need quiet, private desk space
